# T067 — Fragment focus and ownership boundaries

Completed 16 October 2026. Commit: recorded in Git history as the T067 commit.

Fragment focus handling now captures the active element before mutation, leaves surviving permitted focus untouched, and schedules replacement focus after the binding flush. Non-focusable fallbacks receive a temporary `tabindex=-1` that is restored on blur or disposal; canceled owners cannot perform late focus. A shared boundary validator rejects root/router overlap, component internals and host replacement, plus outer swaps around an initiating action by DOM or owner ancestry. Destination-lifetime signals remove sibling insertion ranges without permitting old work to recreate them.

Validation: the six browser acceptance cases passed across Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit (18 passing cases). Two integration contract tests also passed for router/action ownership rejection and explicit component-local allowance. TypeScript passed.

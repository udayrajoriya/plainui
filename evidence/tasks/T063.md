# T063 — Trusted Types and HTML sink isolation

Completed 16 October 2026. Commit: recorded in Git history as the T063 commit.

Production parsing now passes through two private services: `TemplateHtmlService` validates developer-owned component-import grammar with the cached `plainui-template` policy, and `FragmentHtmlService` applies the cached DOMPurify instance and PlainUI sanitation policy to all untrusted values, including caller-supplied TrustedHTML objects. Both services return or insert DOM nodes; policy creation failures are translated to their local diagnostic and occur before live DOM replacement.

Validation: `tt.core`, `tt.import`, `tt.fragment`, `tt.multiroot`, `tt.blocked-policy-preserves`, and `tt.no-extra-sinks` ran under an external-script-only CSP in Chromium, Firefox, and WebKit (18 passing cases). Chromium exercised enforced Trusted Types. The source audit found no production parsing sink outside `core/src/html/template-service.ts` and `fragments/src/html-service.ts`.
